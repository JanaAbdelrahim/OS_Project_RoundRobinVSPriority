SCENARIO D — STARVATION CASE

Description:
This scenario demonstrates starvation risk in Priority Scheduling.

Processes:
------------------------------------------------
Process    Arrival Time    Burst Time    Priority
------------------------------------------------
P1          0               3             1
P2          0               5             1
P3          0               4             2
P4          0               6             5
P5          0               3             5
------------------------------------------------

Round Robin Quantum:
2

Expected Behaviour:
- P5 may wait for a very long time in Priority Scheduling.
- Round Robin should still allow P5 to execute fairly.
- Starvation should be visible in Priority Scheduling.

Purpose:
To demonstrate starvation problems in Priority Scheduling.
