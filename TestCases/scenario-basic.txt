SCENARIO A — BASIC WORKLOAD

Description:
Basic scheduling test with mixed arrival times and burst times.

Processes:
------------------------------------------------
Process    Arrival Time    Burst Time    Priority
------------------------------------------------
P1          0               8             3
P2          1               4             1
P3          2               6             2
P4          3               3             4
P5          4               5             2
------------------------------------------------

Round Robin Quantum:
3

Expected Behaviour:
- Round Robin should distribute CPU time fairly.
- Priority Scheduling should prioritize P2 first.
- Preemptive Priority may interrupt currently running processes.
- Round Robin should prevent starvation.

Purpose:
To test normal scheduling behaviour with mixed workloads.
