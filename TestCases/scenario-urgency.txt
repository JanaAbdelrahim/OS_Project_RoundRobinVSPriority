SCENARIO B — URGENT PROCESSES

Description:
This scenario tests how Priority Scheduling handles urgent tasks.

Processes:
------------------------------------------------
Process    Arrival Time    Burst Time    Priority
------------------------------------------------
P1          0               10            4
P2          1               3             1
P3          2               7             3
P4          3               2             1
P5          4               5             2
------------------------------------------------

Round Robin Quantum:
2

Expected Behaviour:
- Priority Scheduling should immediately prioritize P2 and P5.
- Preemptive Priority may interrupt lower-priority processes.
- Round Robin should still share CPU fairly among all processes.

Purpose:
To compare fairness vs urgency handling.
